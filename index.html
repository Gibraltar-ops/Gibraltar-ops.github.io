About Me

I'm studying Game Creator – Game Programmer at Yrgo in Gothenburg and graduate in June 2027. I've been playing games since I was about five, and what I like about making them is getting behind the scenes and seeing what actually drives them. I especially like physics-based games, since ragdolls and chaos are just a lot of fun.

Before this I studied UX design. I did internships at a medtech company in elderly care, where I worked on an interface staff used to keep track of their tasks, and at Faktum, where I helped steer the UX of a web app for finding your nearest Faktum seller and reading a bit about who they are.

I've also been working as a train driver since 2015. I'd rather work in a smaller team, at an indie or AA studio. I'm based in Gothenburg and can work remotely, but I'm not able to relocate. When I'm not making games, I'm usually out in the forest somewhere.
